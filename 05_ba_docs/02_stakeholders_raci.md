# 2. Stakeholders and RACI

| Stakeholder | What they need | RACI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head of Retail Lending (sponsor) | More disbursals, faster TAT, a clear business case | Accountable |
| Business Analyst | Requirements, analysis, documents, UAT support | Responsible |
| Loan Operations Manager | Fewer re-requests, a clear daily view of pending files | Responsible |
| Credit Risk / Underwriting head | Any fast-track lane stays within risk appetite | Consulted |
| IT / LOS development team | Clear, feasible requirements and acceptance criteria | Responsible |
| Compliance team | KYC and RBI fair-lending rules are followed | Consulted |
| DSA channel manager | DSAs submit complete files the first time | Consulted |
| QA team | Testable requirements, RTM, UAT cases | Consulted |
| Customer service | Fewer "where is my loan?" calls | Informed |
| Loan applicants (end users) | A quick, predictable loan decision | Informed |

## Elicitation plan

| Technique | With whom | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interviews | Sponsor, Operations Manager, Credit head | Goals, constraints, success measures |
| Process walkthrough (observation) | Document verification and underwriting staff | How a file really moves, where it waits |
| Workshop | Credit Risk, Compliance, IT | Rules for a safe fast-track lane |
| Document analysis | Application and stage data, re-request reasons | Where time is lost and why |

> In this case study the stakeholders and interviews are simulated; the data analysis is real analysis of synthetic data.
